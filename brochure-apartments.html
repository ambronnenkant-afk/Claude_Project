Apartment buildings face mold and air quality challenges that unit‑by‑unit cleaning can't solve. Shared HVAC systems, stacked plumbing, and interconnected wall cavities mean a problem in one unit spreads to others before it's ever found. MoldCure's dry‑fog sanitization treats the entire building simultaneously — every unit, corridor, mechanical space, and duct — eliminating mold, spores, and pathogens at the source in a single visit.

What Makes MoldCure Different
  • 🏢

    Whole‑Building Coverage in One Visit

    Our VaPURE™ dry fog reaches shared HVAC ducts, wall cavities, crawl spaces, and common areas — not just individual unit surfaces.

  • 🌬️

    Stops Cross‑Unit Mold Spread

    By treating the entire building at once, we eliminate the shared air pathways and hidden cavities that allow mold to migrate between units.

  • ⚡

    Minimal Resident Disruption

    Treatment is performed after hours or over a weekend. Residents can return the same day — no extended relocation required.

  • 🔬

    GLP Lab‑Validated Technology

  • 💚

    Safe for Residents & Pets

    Once the fog dissipates and the building is aerated, it is safe to re‑enter — no harsh residues or chemical odors.
